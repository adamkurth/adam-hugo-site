## Research experience

### Brown University

<div class="cv-row"><span class="cv-role">Research Assistant, Department of Biostatistics</span><span class="cv-date">Sep 2026 – Present</span></div>

Emulating target trials with Flatiron Health real-world data to evaluate when to discontinue immunotherapy. Implementing inverse probability of treatment and censoring weights (IPTW, IPCW) under baseline and time-varying confounding in a grace-period design driven by adverse events and progression. Designing R simulation studies that vary confounding structure and mismeasurement of time-to-adverse-event to quantify bias in downstream treatment assignment.

### Arizona State University

<div class="cv-row"><span class="cv-role">Research Assistant, School of Mathematical &amp; Statistical Sciences</span><span class="cv-date">Jan – May 2025</span></div>

Advisor: Eleni Panagiotou. Quantified structural entanglement of intrinsically disordered proteins implicated in neurodegenerative disease using topological data analysis and knot-theoretic invariants. Ran molecular simulation studies with Wenwei Zheng across temperature states and molecular configurations.

<div class="cv-row"><span class="cv-role">Research Aide, Decision Theater</span><span class="cv-date">Aug 2024 – Jan 2025</span></div>

<div class="cv-row"><span class="cv-role">Volunteer Student Researcher</span><span class="cv-date">Dec 2023 – May 2024</span></div>

Faculty mentor: Mark Reiser. Conducted the end-to-end data analysis for a Monte Carlo comparison of item response theory (IRT) estimation across Mplus, flexMIRT, SAS PROC IRT, `ltm`, and `mirt`, fitting models in all but SAS and running all analyses in R.

<div class="cv-row"><span class="cv-role">Research Aide, Compact X-ray Free Electron Laser (CXFEL)</span><span class="cv-date">Jun 2023 – Aug 2024</span></div>

Advisor: Sabine Botha. Engineered high-throughput data pipelines in Python, MATLAB, and Bash for serial femtosecond crystallography. Invented a signal-processing algorithm that masks peak intensities to isolate and subtract background noise in low-flux imaging. Co-developed a convolutional neural network to detect Bragg peaks and estimate experimental parameters from raw diffraction images.

### NASA Glenn Research Center

<div class="cv-row"><span class="cv-role">Research Intern, CHP-PRA Team</span><span class="cv-date">Jun – Aug 2024</span></div>

Integrated outputs into a Human Reliability Analysis framework supporting probabilistic risk assessment for deep-space exploration.
