Much of what draws me to biostatistics is the gap between what the evidence says and what reaches patients. Alongside my research I've spent several years on organized outreach in organ donation and transplantation — donor registration, community fundraising, public speaking, and student health advocacy.

## Roles

### Donate Life Arizona <span class="cv-date">Sep 2022 – Aug 2025</span>

**Community Advocate and Public Speaker.** Led on-campus donor registration campaigns through ASU Club Zero and contributed to statewide public health outreach on organ donation. Featured in [Donate Life Arizona's](https://www.donatelifeaz.org/) Campus Challenge, a registration drive reaching college students across the state.

### Children's Organ Transplant Association <span class="cv-date">Apr 2020 – Aug 2025</span>

**Event Coordinator.** Organized operations and execution for *The Mulligan*, an annual golf tournament fundraiser supporting transplant families. [COTA](https://cota.org/) is a national nonprofit that helps families meet the costs of transplantation.

### Brown University Student Health &amp; Wellness Advisory Committee <span class="cv-date">Oct 2025 – Present</span>

**Student Liaison.** Represent student needs to Brown's Health and Wellness leadership, working to align campus health resources with student priorities.

### Starlab ExpertLink <span class="cv-date">Oct 2024</span>

**STEM Expert Panelist.** Presented on space biology, astronaut health, and NASA research initiatives to K–12 students.
